# Page head

The heading of a working screen, above its panels: its name as the page's `h1`, in sans, a count beside it in the pixel face, and the screen's own Buttons at the right, which drop under the title on a phone. One per screen; the panels below it title in `h2`. Crumbs go over it.

::Example{name="with-crumbs"}

## Focus on open

`titleAttributes` go on the `h1`: an id and `tabindex="-1"` make it where focus lands when the screen opens.
