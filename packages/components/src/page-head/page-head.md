# Page head

The heading of a working screen, above its groups: its name as the page's `h1` in a large bold face, its `glyph` before it, the `meta` line under it (the day and a count, or what the screen holds), and the screen's own Buttons at the right, which drop under the title on a phone. A ghost Button with its key hint is the usual action. A `strip` of chips under the head names the goals the screen's tasks serve. One per screen; the groups and panels below it title in `h2`. Crumbs go over it.

## Focus on open

`titleAttributes` go on the `h1`: an id and `tabindex="-1"` make it where focus lands when the screen opens.
