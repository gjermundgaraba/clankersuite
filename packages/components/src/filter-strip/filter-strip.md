# Filter strip

A printed strip of filters above a list, on a 3px shadow: a group named "Filters" unless given another label. It holds `FilterChip`, `QueryChip` and `Segmented` controls; `h.span([h.Class("gg-spacer")])` pushes what follows to the right.

Filters over a list print as chips, pressed when in force. Here are two toggles, a pinned filter, a query, and a reset read in full.
