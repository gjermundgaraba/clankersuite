# Tag

A small muted label on a 1px rule: a count, a group name, a scope.

## Counts and scopes

A tag that is abbreviated or clipped takes its words written out in `full`: screen readers hear `3 in Operations` for `3`, and the application's Tips shows the same words as its tip. With `onClick` a tag is a button.
