# Disclosure

A line that opens what hangs under it, for what a reader opens on purpose: grants to edit, details, a danger zone. Never use it to hide the one thing the screen is for. Whether it is open is the application's: it keeps the state `onToggle` reports and passes it back as `isOpen`.

::Example{name="settings"}

## Open and shut

Open, the caret turns a quarter in one step and the body hangs from it on a 1px rule. Shut, the body stays on the page, hidden, so a field in it keeps what was typed. Enter and Space on the line toggle it, as a press does.
