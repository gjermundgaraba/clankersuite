# Sheet

An authored page, an `<article>` under its `h1` on its own sheet of paper, framed as a panel is: the one place the system slows to reading pace. Its byline says who changed it and when, in the pixel face over a 1px rule, and its Buttons sit at the title's right. Its body is a Prose.

::Example{name="checklist"}

## In a page

A sheet stands in a panel's place, never inside one, so its `h1` opens the page instead of following a panel's `h2`.
