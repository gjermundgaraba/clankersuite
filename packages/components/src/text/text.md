# Text

Quiet words in one of the system's two secondary voices. `Meta` is the UI face at label size, beside a title or a name: an address, a count, "shown once". `Note` is the body face, kept to a note's measure, for a line under or between parts: an empty list's "No matching tasks", a hint under a form.

A `Meta` prints inline and a `Note` as a paragraph; `isInline` turns either. Panel, Card, PageHead and Identity print their `meta` in the `Meta` voice themselves.
