# Outcome

A state said in full: a 34px glyph box, a title in pixel caps, one sentence on why and what to do next. It is the body of a confirm, and of a wall that stops the reader: session ended, access refused, service away.

## Tones

`Warn`, the default, is amber: a question or a stop. `Error` is red: refused or failed. `Ok` is a committed change, in ink. `Quiet` is tinted: nothing wrong.

::Example{name="tones"}

## Detail

What follows the sentence: a code, the details, a list of what is asked.
