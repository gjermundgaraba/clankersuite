# Title field

A title edited where it is printed: a bold headline that is its own field, so a task's title is never printed twice, once to read and once to edit. It wraps and grows as the headline would, and sits on a tint while it is hovered or focused. Enter is done editing and never breaks the line. Its name is for screen readers, since the title labels itself. The Inbox card and a side pane's task print one.
